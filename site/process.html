Four stages, in order, with founder eyes on every one. No hidden junior hand-offs.

Diagnose, define, design, run.

  1. Week 1

    Diagnose.

    We audit before we touch anything: your listings and profiles, the site, the packaging, the last ninety days of ads. You get one page on what is costing you, ranked by what it costs.

    Output →One-page audit, ranked
  2. Weeks 2–3

    Define.

    We decide what to say and who it's for. Positioning, name system, how prices are presented. Everything after this gets checked against it, including our own work.

    Output →A brief the founder signs
  3. Weeks 3–8

    Design.

    Identity, signage, packaging, the site, the shoot. Every piece is redlined twice before it leaves: kerning, colour, the frame a reel cuts on, the second line of a caption.

    Output →Files, systems, a guide people use
  4. Monthly

    Run.

    We launch, then measure what matters to you: leads, sales, conversion, return on ad spend, cost per acquisition. Reported every month, in plain language, with the next fix named.

    Output →A monthly numbers review
