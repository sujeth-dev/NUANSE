05 · Case · Zingara

The menu was costing them.

189 items, four typefaces and prices that wandered. Diners spent their attention finding dishes instead of wanting them. We kept every dish and changed how the page reads.

  1. AOne display face, kerned by hand. The name reads as a place, not a font sample.
  2. BOxblood as the only accent. Four competing colours became one decision.
  3. CPrices in one column. No “Rs.”, no “/-”. Dishes first, numbers second.

07 · Process

How it runs.

Four stages, in order, with founder eyes on every one. No hidden junior hand-offs.

  1. Stage 01Week 1

    Diagnose.

    We audit before we touch anything: the menu, your listings on Swiggy and Zomato, the site, the last ninety days of ads. You get one page on what is costing you, ranked by what it costs.

    Output →One-page audit, ranked
  2. Stage 02Weeks 2–3

    Define.

    We decide what to say and who it's for. Positioning, name system, how prices are presented. Everything after this gets checked against it, including our own work.

    Output →A brief the founder signs
  3. Stage 03Weeks 3–8

    Design.

    Identity, menus, packaging, the site, the shoot. Every piece is redlined twice before it leaves: kerning, colour, the frame a reel cuts on, the second line of a caption.

    Output →Files, systems, a guide people use
  4. Stage 04Monthly

    Run.

    We launch, then measure what matters to you: orders, conversion, return on ad spend, cost per reservation. Reported every month, in plain language, with the next fix named.

    Output →A monthly numbers review
