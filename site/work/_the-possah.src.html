01 · Built, not booked

Twenty-four pieces, no studio.

A studio day buys a location, a crew, a model and light that does not move. The budget bought none of it, so the campaign was built to a direction instead.

Models and rooms generated · the garments are real · 24 SKUs, as recorded on NUANSE's current site

04 · Naming

A grammar, not a list.

Couture produces a new collection faster than a new brand. So the names got a rule, and the label four sub-lines that each know what they are for.

05 · It travels

Where it is touched.

A couture piece is bought in a room and opened at home. Both are the brand, and the second one is the one people photograph.

06 · On a screen

Found in a feed.

A couture piece is found in a grid and ordered on a phone. The feed is planned a month at a time, and the site was audited before it was redesigned.
